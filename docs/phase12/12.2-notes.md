# Phase 12.2 – Defense

Written and run in Godot 4.7.2 (headless, Linux): **8 new checks, all passing** (section BBB, BBB1 to BBB8). Expected total after this step: previous total + 8.

## Why
Damage reduction had no home. It is the first rule added to `CombatResolver2D.resolve()`, which proves that 12.1's single place works.

## What changed
- `HealthComponent2D.gd`: new export `defense: int` (0 to 1000, default 0), shown in the Inspector on the component.
- `CombatResolver2D.gd`: `resolve()` subtracts defense. Its second argument is now the `HealthComponent2D` (it was the component's parent in 12.1), because that is where `defense` lives.
- `HealthComponent2D.apply_hit()`: passes `self` to the resolver.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: section BBB.

No scene or `.tres` changed: every shipped character keeps `defense = 0`.

## The formula
```
damage_after_defense = max(damage - defense, 1)
```
Flat subtraction, with a minimum of 1 (`CombatResolver2D.MIN_DAMAGE`). A hit that can land always does at least 1. A hit with 0 damage is still ignored before the resolver runs. Status ticks skip defense (12.4).

## Decisions
- **Defense lives on `HealthComponent2D`, not on a stats resource.** It is the only stat the resolver reads from the target today, the component already owns max health and invulnerability, and a character's tuning is already done on that node in the Scene dock. A stats resource can replace it when a second stat needs the same treatment.
- **Flat, not percent.** Health is a small integer (the player has 5, a goblin 20) and damage is a whole number, so a percentage would round to nothing on most hits.
- **The resolved hit is what `damaged` carries and what `apply_hit()` returns.** Effects report the damage actually dealt in `hit_confirmed`, so a hit effect shows the reduced number.

## The checks (section BBB)
| Check | What it proves |
|---|---|
| BBB1, BBB2 | The shipped characters have defense 0, and with 0 the resolver returns the very same hit. |
| BBB3 | Defense 2 lowers a 5 hit to 3; the **returned** hit is the one applied and `damaged` carries it, and the original hit is not changed. (This is the check 12.1 listed as not testable yet.) |
| BBB4, BBB5 | Damage never drops below 1; defense equal to the damage deals 1; 4 gives 1 and 6 gives 3 with defense 3. |
| BBB6 | A hit with no damage is still ignored. |
| BBB7, BBB8 | A melee slash (5) and a projectile (3) on a target with defense 2 deal 3 and 1. |

## To use it in the editor
Open a character scene, select the `HealthComponent2D` node, and set **Defense** in the Inspector.
