# Phase 13.2 - `SkillComponent2D` and `ManaComponent2D`

Written and run in Godot 4.7.2 (headless, Linux): **26 new checks** (EEE9 to EEE34). Expected total after 13.1 and 13.2: previous total + 34 = **760 passes, 0 failures** (726 baseline).

## What changed
- `Characters/Components/Resources/ManaComponent2D.gd` (new): `max_mana`, `regen_per_second`, `mana`, `can_afford()`, `spend()`, `restore()`, signal `mana_changed`. It regenerates by itself and only processes while below the maximum.
- `Characters/Components/Skills/SkillComponent2D.gd` (new node): `set_skills()`, `can_use(id)` (the reason a skill is refused, or an empty string), `try_use_skill(id, direction)`, `try_use_slot(slot)`, `get_cooldown_remaining()`, `is_ready()`, `is_casting()`, `interrupt()`, `cancel()`; signals `skill_started`, `skill_executed`, `skill_cancelled`. The rules are in `13.0-notes.md`. Until 13.3 a skill that executes only emits `skill_executed`.
- `CharacterContext2D.gd`: `is_casting`. `AttackComponent2D` (`begin_charge`, `_begin_attack`) and `DashComponent2D.try_start` refuse while it is true.
- `Character2D.gd`: optional exports `mana_component` and `skill_component`, found as children named `ManaComponent2D` and `SkillComponent2D`; a damaging non-periodic hit calls `skill_component.interrupt()`, death calls `cancel()`.
- `WeaponController2D.gd`: gives the equipped weapon's `active_skills` to the skill component; equipping is refused during a cast (separate message).

## Checks (EEE9 to EEE34)
Mana: spend, refused spend, restore clamp, regeneration (EEE9, EEE10). Skills: `set_skills` keeps the valid ones (EEE11); an instant skill pays, starts its cooldown and executes (EEE12); cooldown refuses a second use and the skill works again afterwards (EEE13, EEE14); not enough mana and no mana pool fail cleanly (EEE15, EEE16); dashing, attacking and stunned prevent a skill (EEE17); unknown id, empty slot, slot use (EEE18); a cast skill pays and starts its cooldown at cast start (EEE19); while casting no attack, charge, dash, skill or weapon switch (EEE20); the cast executes once (EEE21, EEE22); a hit cancels it, refunds and clears the cooldown (EEE23, EEE24); a periodic hit does not (EEE25, EEE26); `interruptible` off survives (EEE27, EEE28); a weapon gives its skills and cooldowns survive a switch (EEE29 to EEE31); death cancels a cast (EEE32, EEE33); the child nodes are found by name (EEE34).

## To use it in the editor
1. Open `player.tscn`, right-click the root, **Add Child Node**, add `ManaComponent2D` and `SkillComponent2D` (keep the names). Save.
2. Open a weapon definition (for example `Data/Weapons/SwordWeaponDefinition.tres`), open **Skills > Active Skills**, add elements and create new `SkillDefinition2D` resources.
Nothing happens in play yet: input is 13.4 and the behaviors 13.3. Do not add these nodes to the shipped scenes until a skill exists, because the checks assume the shipped characters have none (EEE1).
