## Behavior

- `projectile_count = 1` and `spread_degrees = 0` are the defaults, preserving existing Power Shot behavior. For a volley, `spread_degrees` is the full angle between the two outer projectiles. The fan is evenly distributed around the aim direction; the center shot is aligned with the aim when the count is odd.
- Each projectile is a separate effect and follows the existing team filter, hit-once policy, hit data, impact effects, and skill-hit signal. Cost and cooldown are paid/started once per skill activation, not once per arrow.
- Multi-shot's embedded attack deals 4 damage per arrow and is non-piercing with one target maximum per arrow.
- Sword Spin uses the existing `AreaEffect2D` scene, targets `SELF`, and centers the area on the character rather than a weapon spawn marker. The shared attack-effect team filter prevents the caster from being damaged.
- Ground Slam remains a valid skill definition for independent uses/tests, but is no longer equipped on the sword; slot 1 is now Spin as specified by the roadmap.
- The dash-slash variant of Lunge is deferred until the user can play-test whether the current dash needs an added attack effect.
