## Behavior

- The HUD contains three reusable `SkillSlot.tscn` instances, not code-constructed controls.
- `SkillComponent2D` remains the single authority for skill availability, costs, casts and cooldowns. The bar listens to `skills_changed` and refreshes the three bindings.
- Cooldowns remain keyed by skill id in `SkillComponent2D`; a weapon switch changes what each slot displays but does not reset the old weapon's cooldown. Switching back binds the old skill again and its wipe reads the remaining time.
- An empty weapon slot shows its frame and hotkey, with no icon, cost or cooldown overlay.
- Key labels are derived from key events in `InputMap`. Because `InputMap` does not expose a change signal, the bar compares the three labels while processing and updates only labels that changed.
- The bar is a child of the independent `HUD` CanvasLayer, so it remains present if the player stops processing on death.
