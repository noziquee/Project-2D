## What you can do in the game now
| Key | Sword | Bow |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lunge: dash about 120 px toward the mouse with a strike, free, 4 s cooldown | Power Shot: an arrow that can hit up to 3 targets, 20 mana, 4 s cooldown |
| 2 | Ground Slam: a circle in front of you, 15 mana, 6 s cooldown | nothing (empty slot) |

Mana is 100 and regenerates 5 per second, so Ground Slam costs 3 s of regeneration and Power Shot 4 s. Empowerment is the same skill on both weapons, so its cooldown carries over when you press Q. Tune these numbers after you play.

## Decisions and notes
- **Icons are `GradientTexture2D`, not SVG.** The plan said placeholder SVGs. A new `.svg` has no import data until the editor (or `godot --headless --import`) creates it, and a skill whose `icon` fails to load would stop the weapon loading in a plain smoke-test run. A gradient texture is a built-in resource and always loads. Each icon is a 64 x 64 radial gradient in its own colour (red Lunge, brown Ground Slam, gold Empowerment, green Power Shot). Real art replaces them later by pointing the skill's `icon` at a PNG or SVG; nothing else needs to change.
- **No weapon clips needed.** The plan asked to check each skill's `weapon_animation` against the clips of `Sword.tscn` and `Bow.tscn`. None of the four shipped skills sets one, so nothing was missing and no clip was reused. The skills have no cast animation yet (Phase 15).
- **Shared resource.** `EmpowerSkillDefinition.tres` is listed by both weapons. It is data only; cooldown and cost state live in the player's `SkillComponent2D`, which is why the cooldown survives a weapon switch (rule from 13.0).
- **The goblin archer uses the bow definition**, so it now carries the bow's `active_skills` too. It has no `SkillComponent2D`, so the weapon controller never hands them over and nothing changes for it.
