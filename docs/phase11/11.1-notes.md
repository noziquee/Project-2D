## How it behaves
- It flies along `direction` at `speed` px/s (default 300) for at most `lifetime` seconds (default 1.5). Both are exports on the scene root for now. 11.2 adds `projectile_speed` to the attack definition and passes it in.
- Hits go through `AttackEffect2D._try_hit()`: not the shooter, `damageable` group, not the same team, once per target, `HitData` into `apply_hit()`. `hit_contact` and `hit_confirmed` are emitted as for a slash, so `AttackComponent2D` already spawns the attack's `hit_effect_scene` at the contact point.
- Hit policy from 10.4: `max_targets` and `pierce` end the projectile when used up (`_on_spent()` frees it). Defaults (no limit, pierce on) mean it flies through every enemy until its lifetime ends; set `pierce = false` on the attack for a normal arrow.
- Targets come from the attack's `hit_layers`. The world bit (value 4) is always added to the hit area's mask, so a wall stops the projectile even when `hit_layers` is 3. A body on the world layer that is **not** in the `damageable` group stops it; one that is damageable is hit first and the pierce rules apply.
- On a world hit it emits `world_hit(point)` and frees itself.
- The scene's node paths are exported (`hitbox_path`, `collision_shape_path`), defaulting to `Hitbox` and `Hitbox/CollisionShape2D`, as in 10.3. A broken path logs an error and the projectile removes itself.
- `hitbox_size` resizes the rectangle (default 16 x 16, or what the attack sets), `hitbox_offset` moves the hit area in the rotated frame, and the root is rotated to the flight direction.
- `effect_animation` is ignored (no effect `AnimationPlayer`). `validate_definition()` does not judge a scene without one, so a projectile scene passes it.

## Limits and decisions
- **Fast projectiles can tunnel.** Overlaps are checked once per physics frame. At 60 fps, keep `speed / 60` below the hitbox length (the default 300 px/s moves 5 px a frame, a 16 px box). If the bow needs very high speeds, a ray cast along the step is the fix; not built, because nothing needs it yet.
- **No impact effect at a wall.** `hit_contact` carries a target, so the attack's `hit_effect_scene` is only spawned at hits on characters. A wall impact would need `AttackComponent2D` to listen to `world_hit`, which this step avoids on purpose. Decide in 11.2 or 11.3 if the arrow should puff on walls.
- **The placeholder arrow** is a `Polygon2D`; replace it with a sprite in 11.2 (the arrow scene inherits this one).
- **Spawn position.** `AttackComponent2D` sets `global_position` after the node is in the tree, so the projectile does not read its position in `_ready()`; the first overlap check happens in the next physics frame.
