# Phase 11.5 – Goblin archer

Run in Godot 4.7.2 (headless): **`SUMMARY passes=680 failures=0`** (669 before this step + **11 checks**, section ZZ: ZZ1 to ZZ11).

## Why
The last proof of Phase 11: a character that is not the player uses the bow's arrow, through the same attack component, with no change to any component or to the AI controller.

## What changed (content only, plus one arena entry)
- `Characters/Enemies/GoblinArcher.tscn` (new): an **inherited scene** of `BasicEnemy2D.tscn`. It replaces the knife with the bow sprite (scaled to 0.6 for the 16 px goblin), gives the weapon `AnimationPlayer` its own library (`RESET`, `bow_shoot`, `bow_return`), sets the attack component to the new arrow attack with no extra attacks (the goblin's lunge is dropped), uses its own behaviour profile and has 12 health (a goblin has 20).
- `Characters/Components/Combat/GoblinArrowAttackDefinition.tres` (new, `goblin_arrow`): damage 1 (the player has 5 health), cooldown 1.2 s, arrow speed 200 px/s (slow enough to dodge), knockback 80, hit stun 0.1, `pierce` off, `interruptible` on, `ai_attack_range` 170. It uses `Arrow.tscn`.
- `Characters/Controllers/GoblinArcherBehaviorProfile.tres` (new): one attack choice, detection radius 260 (the goblin's circle stays at 200), disengage radius 320.
- `Main.tscn`: one `GoblinArcher` at (330, 110), on the empty east side of the arena.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: section ZZ.

## Behaviour
- The archer notices the player at 260 px, walks in until the player is inside 170 px, stops, draws for 0.4 s (the bow shrinks as it is drawn, the telegraph) and releases. About 1.8 s between shots.
- A player already inside 170 px is shot where the archer stands: the archer does not walk closer.
- Arrows cross goblins and other archers (same team), hit the player once, and vanish on walls.
- No change to `EnemyAIController2D`: it already stops and swings once the target is inside the chosen attack's `ai_attack_range`, so a long range is all a ranged enemy needs. The `Behavior` enum was left alone, as planned.

## Limits (decided not to build yet)
- **No kiting.** The archer does not back away when the player closes in. It keeps shooting at point-blank range. A retreat behaviour would be its own small AI step (a new `Behavior` value or a profile field), and nothing needs it yet.
- **No line of sight.** It shoots even with a wall in the way; the arrow just stops at the wall.
- **The bow does not aim.** The goblin rig only mirrors left or right (`aim_weapon` is ignored by it), so the bow always points horizontally while the arrow flies at the player. Looks wrong when the player is above or below. A rotating weapon on the goblin rig would fix it; that is rig work (Track P, P2).
- **No arrow impact on walls**, and no arrow aimed ahead of a moving player (it shoots at where the player is).
- The look (the bow's pose and scale on the goblin, the draw telegraph) was not seen in the running game. Check it in V3.
