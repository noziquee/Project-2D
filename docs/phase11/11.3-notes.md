# Phase 11.3 – Bow weapon scene and definition

Run in Godot 4.7.2 (headless): the full run is `SUMMARY passes=669 failures=0` (see 11.2 notes). This step's share is **14 checks** (section XX: XX1 to XX11, with XX7pre, XX9pre and XX9pre2).

## Why
The bow proves that a different kind of weapon needs no change to `AttackComponent2D`, `WeaponController2D` or the character scenes. The only code in 11.2 to 11.4 that touches the attack component is the one extra argument of 11.2.

## What changed (content only)
- `Weapons/Bow.tscn` (new): root `MainHand` (`Node2D`, `z_index` 1), a `weapon` sprite (`Bow.png`) and `HandAnim` with six clips. The `attack_event` method tracks use the path `../..`, as the sword's do, so the scene must sit directly under `WeaponFacingPivot`.
- `Data/Weapons/BowWeaponDefinition.tres` (new): `weapon_id` `bow`, `basic_combo` = `[arrow_shot]` (one shot, no combo), `heavy_attack` = `arrow_heavy`.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: section XX.

## The clips
| Clip | Length | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `RESET`, `idle` | tiny | Rest pose: position (-4, 5), rotation -45 degrees. |
| `bow_shoot` | 0.4 s | Raises the bow to (8, 1) by 0.12 s, `spawn_effect` at 0.2 s (the release), recoil to (6, 1). |
| `bow_charge` | 0.2 s, looping | Holds the draw at (8, 1) with a 0.5 px tremble. Used while the heavy attack charges. |
| `bow_heavy_shoot` | 0.3 s | `spawn_effect` at 0.04 s (the draw already happened during the charge), recoil to (5, 1). |
| `bow_return` | 0.3 s | Back to rest. Recovery clip of both attacks. |

Only position is animated. The bow sprite faces lower-right in the art (its belly is the front), so the scene's rest rotation is -45 degrees and the sprite points along +X in the bow's own frame; the player's `aim_weapon` rotation then points it at the cursor. Checked by rendering the sprite with that transform. Not seen in the running game: do it in V3 (look at the bow while aiming up, down and left, and at the draw and recoil).

## Behaviour
- The arrow spawns at the character's origin (no `AttackSpawnPoint` yet), travelling along the attack direction. The hit area starts 8 px ahead (`hitbox_offset`).
- Light shot: draw, release, recoil, return, about 0.7 s per shot plus the 0.05 s cooldown.
- Heavy shot: hold to charge (the bow holds its draw), release fires the piercing arrow.
- With `release_mode` INPUT_RELEASE, as the sword's heavy.
