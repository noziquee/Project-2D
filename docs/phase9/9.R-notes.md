- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: player clip names are read from the definitions instead of hard-coded (`weapon_cycle_seconds`, A4, J2, aliases in I and J), or updated (I1, AA, BB1, EE1, EE3). `definition_variant()` clears `next_attack` and `combo_sequence` so test variants never inherit the shipped link.

  - **J2** counts only `spawn_effect` events (the light clips also carry window keys).
