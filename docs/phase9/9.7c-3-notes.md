## Root cause this step solves
Two things were fixed to the first weapon: `AttackComponent2D.initialize()` connected `animation_finished` once to the player it found, and `context.weapon_animation_player` was read by name at start-up. A new weapon would have swung but never finished its attack. Now the component tracks which player it listens to and can rebind.

## Changed files
- `Characters/Components/Combat/AttackComponent2D.gd`: new `rebind_weapon_player()` and a `_connected_player` field. It disconnects the old player and connects the context's current one; `initialize()` now calls it instead of connecting inline. Behaviour at start-up is the same.
- `Characters/Components/Weapons/WeaponController2D.gd`:
  - `equip()` swaps the scene in when the weapon has a `weapon_scene` that is not the mounted one (`_needs_swap`). A weapon with no scene, or the mounted scene, keeps the weapon that is there (9.7c-2 behaviour).
  - `_swap_mount(new_mount)`: removes the old mount from the tree (not freed yet), gives the new one the old name and child index, adds it under `WeaponFacingPivot`, `bind_weapon()`, `rebind_weapon_player()`. Paths like `WeaponFacingPivot/MainHand` stay valid.
  - Then the attacks are applied (the clips are checked against the new weapon's player, so the order is bind first, attacks second). On success the old mount is freed at once, so it cannot keep the name.
  - On failure after the swap, `_restore_mount()` frees the new weapon and puts the old one back, rebinds, and only then are the old attacks restored (they are validated against the old player, so the mount must come back first). The old pieces were split into `_snapshot_attacks()`, `_set_attacks_from()` and `_restore_attacks()`; `_apply_attacks()` still exists and behaves as in 9.7c-2.
  - New refusal: the mounted weapon must be a direct child of `WeaponFacingPivot`.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: stub class `FailingApplyController` at the top, section MM at the end, and **LL6a to LL6c removed on purpose** (they expected a different scene to be refused naming 9.7c-3; that is now MM1 to MM2).
- `docs/phase9/PLAN.md`.

## Decisions
- The new weapon takes the old one's **name and position**, not its own root name, so scene paths used by the game and tests do not change.
- Nothing is reset: equipping is still refused during a swing or charge, and the cooldown keeps running.
- The old weapon is freed only after everything succeeded, so a failed equip never loses it.

## Tests (section MM, 19 checks)
MM1 a packed copy of the sword scene validates and equips; MM2 old weapon freed, new one has the old name, index and path, context reads its player; MM3 attacks registered; MM4 a swing on the new weapon spawns its effect and finishes (proves the signal was rebound and `attack_event` still reaches the character); MM5 swapping back to the shipped sword; MM6 refused during a swing; MM7 a failing attack step (stub) restores the old mount, context, attacks and signal (a swing finishes afterwards).

MM1a is a setup check. If it fails, the packed-copy trick is the problem, not the swap; send me that line.
