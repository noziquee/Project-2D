## New files
- `Characters/Components/Weapons/WeaponDefinition2D.gd` (a Resource, `class_name WeaponDefinition2D`)
  - Fields: `weapon_id`, `display_name`, `weapon_scene` (optional until 9.7b), `basic_combo: Array[AttackDefinition2D]`, `heavy_attack`.
  - Helpers: `get_default_attack()` (first non-empty combo entry), `get_extra_attacks()` (the rest of the combo, then the heavy: the shape `AttackComponent2D.attacks` expects), `get_all_attacks()`, `get_attack(id)`, `get_attack_ids()`.
  - `validate(animation_player = null) -> Array[String]`, empty when valid. Without a player it checks the data; with one it also checks every attack's `weapon_animation`, `recovery_animation` and `charge_animation` exist. Every message names the weapon.
- `Data/Weapons/SwordWeaponDefinition.tres`: `sword`, combo `player_light` then `player_light2`, heavy `player_heavy`. It points at the shipped attack `.tres` files, so tuning an attack still happens in one place.

## What validate() reports
- empty `weapon_id`; no attack in `basic_combo`; an empty entry; an empty or duplicate attack id (combo and heavy share one id space, like `attacks` on the component);
- a `next_attack` or `combo_sequence` entry that is not part of the weapon (a self link is valid: it means repeat);
- a combo entry that no `next_attack` / `combo_sequence` chain from the first attack reaches (it could never be chained into);
- missing clips (with an AnimationPlayer), and a recovery clip equal to the weapon clip.
