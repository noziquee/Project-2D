## What changed
- `Characters/Components/Weapons/WeaponController2D.gd` (the only script changed)
  - `equip(weapon) -> bool`, `get_equipped_weapon()`, signals `weapon_equipped(weapon)` and `equip_failed(weapon, problems)`.
  - Order: `validate_equip()`, then a mount check, then the attacks are applied. Any failure emits `equip_failed`, logs each problem as a warning, and keeps the previous weapon.
  - Applying: the weapon's first combo attack becomes the default attack (`set_attack_definition`), then the rest of the combo plus the heavy become `attacks` (`set_attacks`). If either is rejected, the old default and the old list are put back.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: section LL appended. Nothing existing changed.
- `docs/phase9/PLAN.md`.

## Decisions and why
- **Mounted weapons only.** A weapon with no `weapon_scene`, or whose scene is the one mounted (`MainHand`'s `scene_file_path`), can be equipped. Any other scene is refused with a message naming 9.7c-3.
- **No rewiring in this step.** I planned to reconnect `animation_finished` here, but the weapon player does not change when the mounted weapon is kept, so there is nothing to rewire. It moves to 9.7c-3, where the player really changes.
- **Nothing is reset.** Equipping is refused during a swing or charge, so the combo and charge state are already clear. The cooldown keeps running on purpose: switching weapons is not a free cooldown reset.
- **The authored `attack_definition` export is not changed**, only the runtime `definition` and the `attacks` list. Scene data stays as authored.
- A no-scene weapon is checked against the clips on the weapon the character holds now, so equipping one whose clips do not exist there is refused up front.

## Tests (section LL, 20 checks)
LL1 the sword equips and leaves the player's attacks identical; LL2 a code-built "duelist" replaces the attacks, can be swung (effect spawns, swing ends, movement restored) and its heavy can be charged; LL3 the sword equips again and the duelist's attacks are gone; LL4 refused during a swing, nothing changes; LL5 an attack the component rejects restores the old attacks (checked on `_apply_attacks` directly, then through `equip()`); LL6 a weapon with a different scene is refused naming 9.7c-3.

LL6a is a precondition: it packs a copy of the sword scene in code. If LL6a fails, the test setup is the problem, not the controller; send me the line.
