## What changed
- `player.tscn`
  - New clip `heavy_slash` (in the HandAnim library): a slower version of the light swing. Length 0.5 s, the rotation takes 0.42 s, and the hit (`spawn_effect`) is at 0.3 s. It has no combo window keys.
  - `AttackComponent2D.attacks` now lists the combo follow-up and the heavy attack.
- New `Characters/Components/Combat/PlayerHeavyAttackDefinition.tres`: id `player_heavy`, damage 12, cooldown 0.2 s, movement_multiplier 0.4, clip `heavy_slash`, aimed, knockback 320, hit stun 0.3 s, hitbox 28×24. It uses the same effect and hit scenes as the light attack, so the visual is the same slash (a separate heavy effect scene can come later).
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`
  - S1 and S2: the player now lists two extra attacks and three selectable ids. These expectations changed on purpose.
  - T and U (the input sections) clear the player's extra attacks at their start. They were written before a heavy attack existed and test the no-heavy path, so they keep testing it.
  - New section BB (BB1–BB6b): registration and validation, heavy numbers, a real hold through `Input.action_press` that starts the heavy attack, movement restored afterwards, a real tap that still starts the light attack, and the heavy hitting a goblin in reach for its own damage.

## Behaviour change to know about in the play-test
- With a heavy attack registered, a light tap is sent on **release** (9.2b), not on press. The same applies to a combo follow-up. A follow-up tap lands inside the window only if the release does: check the window (0.12 s to 0.26 s into the light swing) against your own tap length. `heavy_hold_time` (0.25 s) is the knob.

## Play-test checklist
1. No errors in the Output panel about `heavy_slash`, `player_heavy` or the heavy definition.
2. Quick tap: light swing. Hold past about 0.25 s: heavy swing, and the character slows while it plays.
3. The heavy hits harder, pushes the goblin further, and the swing is longer than the light one.
4. Tune the heavy's timing in the Animation panel (the hit key at 0.3 s) if it feels early or late.
