## Texture authoring guidance

Use square white or grey textures on transparent backgrounds. Circle/ring art should be centred. Fan art should point right (the current aim direction rotates the shape). Assign the texture in the Inspector; leave it empty for the current vector fallback. Keep important art within the image bounds.
