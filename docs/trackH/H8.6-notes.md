## Rules (validation)
- **Problems** (the weapon is refused on equip, as for any other problem): a shape on a BUFF or NONE skill; an aimed shape (fan, line) on a skill with targeting SELF (only the circle around the caster is allowed); a shape with invalid values (range or angle or width at 0); the same for the heavy attack's shape.
- **Warnings** (logged, never block): the shape's reach differs from the skill's own by more than 25% (PROJECTILE and DASH compare with `max_range`; an AREA skill with a circle compares with `area_radius`); a basic_combo attack sets a shape (only the heavy attack uses one).
- A missing shape on a damage skill is **not** a runtime warning (it would flag every test skill). `wants_aim_shape()` says which skills should have one, and H8-104 proves every shipped one does.

## Decisions made here (tell me if you disagree)
1. The Spin skill (targeting SELF, an area) gets a **circle around the caster**, per D17; the earlier H8.1 idea of "no shape for SELF skills" was dropped. Only buffs have none.
2. Shape sizes are starting values that mirror each skill's own reach. To tune one in the editor: FileSystem dock, open the skill's `.tres`, then Inspector > "Aim Shape" and edit its values. For a heavy attack, open `Sword_HeavySlash.tres` or `Bow_ArrowHeavy.tres` > "Aim Assist" > "Aim Shape". If you change a skill's `max_range`, change the shape's to match.
3. The goblin's and the enemy bow's attacks get no shape (enemy AI aims itself).
