## Rules pinned
- A shape guides aiming only; it never changes the real hitbox or damage.
- `margin` is the tested body's radius, so a big body just outside the edge still counts.
- Directional shapes hold nothing when given no direction; the circle around the caster works with none.
- Score is -1 outside, 0 to 1 inside; a target ahead beats a nearer one off to the side.
- Ground circle (drag-to-place) is declared in `AimShape2D.Kind` only; its class comes with H8.8.
