# H2.2: a skill that names a clip plays it, then executes (decision D3)

Written Oct 27, 2026. Not run: no Godot in the sandbox. Expected `SUMMARY passes=1198 failures=0` (1192 + 6 new checks, H2-7 to H2-12).

## The gap

`SkillDefinition2D.weapon_animation` was checked by `validate()` (the weapon must have the clip) but nothing ever played it. The decision on record (D3) is "cast, then execute" for a skill that names a clip, and nothing for one that does not.

## What changed

- `SkillComponent2D`: when a skill starts it plays its `weapon_animation` (if it names one and the weapon player has it), then executes after `cast_time` exactly as before. An instant skill (`cast_time` 0) plays the clip and executes at once.
- When the clip ends, the weapon goes back to its RESET pose, unless an attack has taken the weapon over. A cancelled cast (an interrupt, death, `cancel()`) stops the clip and does the same. Where `context.hand_settling` is set, the pose is kept, as `AttackComponent2D` does.
- A clip the weapon lacks plays nothing and the skill still runs (`validate()` is what reports it).
- `SkillDefinition2D`: the doc comment on `weapon_animation` now says this (it used to say "played when the skill executes").
- New part `h2_skill_clip` with H2-7 to H2-12.

## Not changed

- No shipped skill names a clip, so nothing in the game plays differently yet. A skill gets its clip when you set `weapon_animation` on it.
- The weapon returns to rest with a snap (RESET), not the blended settle attacks get from FacingComponent2D. That would need FacingComponent2D to listen for skills; worth doing only once a real skill clip exists and looks wrong.
- A clip shorter than `cast_time` leaves the weapon at rest before the effect fires; a longer one keeps playing after it. No warning for either yet.
