## How it works
- **Skills:** the direction is chosen once, at the key press, from the skill's `aim_shape` (a fan, line or arrow around the aim). The cast keeps it (`SkillComponent2D` already stores the direction when a cast time is set), so nothing is retargeted during the cast. A SELF skill (Spin) and a buff (Empower) get no assist: their direction is ignored.
- **Heavy attacks:** while the button is held, the weapon follows the live aim as before (mouse, stick or, from H8.8, a drag). At release the player asks the targeting service with the heavy attack's shape, so the swing or shot goes toward a target inside the shape, else along the aim. A manual aim (stick pushed, or a drag) skips the search.
- **"Bow heavy accepts aim changes while charging, within the shape":** read as: the aim can change freely while charging (it already could), and the assist only acts at release, inside the shape. Tell me if you meant the live aim itself should be limited to the shape.
- Cooldown, mana and combo rules are untouched: only the direction handed to them changes.

## One behavior change (all devices)
The bow's heavy shot plays its draw (0.5 s) before it can fire. If you released during the draw, the shot used to follow the live aim until the draw ended. Now the direction is fixed at release, so an assisted choice is not overwritten and a mouse player gets the aim they had when they let go. Pinned by H8-117. If you prefer the old feel for non-assisted releases, say so.
