# H8.9: aim indicators (October 28, 2026)

Status: built and **run headless on Godot 4.7: `SUMMARY passes=1453 failures=2`** (full suite). The 2 failures are H7-13 and H7-16 (camera zoom and the 9:16 portrait window), which fail the same way on the untouched H8.8 archive in this environment; nothing in H8.9 touches them. 43 new checks, H8-140 to H8-182, in the new part `h8_indicator`. Not yet seen on a screen: the colours, ring size and fill strength are untested by eye (they are constants at the top of `AimIndicator2D.gd`).

## What you get
- **An indicator for every aimed action** (D17): while a heavy attack charges (any device), and while a finger rests on an aimed skill's touch button, the action's `aim_shape` is drawn round the player: a wedge for a fan, a strip for a line, a circle for a circle on self. An ARROW line also gets a head.
- **Same shape as the assist.** The outline comes from the shape resource itself (`AimShape2D.get_outline(direction)`), and a check compares the drawn polygon with `contains()` over a grid for the fan, the 360 degree fan, the line and the circle (H8-143, 144, 146, 150). If someone changes a shape's maths, the picture and the assist cannot drift apart unnoticed.
- **Targets ringed.** Every eligible target inside the shape gets a thin yellow ring; the one the action will go for gets a thicker red ring, and the shape points at it. This takes open question 2 as proposed (highlight enemies inside the shape).
- **Manual aim shows as manual.** While a drag (or a pushed stick) aims the action, the shape follows the finger and no target is picked, exactly as the release will behave.
- **No indicator** for a light tap (D14), a buff or a self skill (D16), a dead player, or with `aim_indicators` off on the player.
- **The cast goes where it showed.** H8-175 compares the indicator's direction at the lift with the cast's direction.

## Found while building
1. **Real bug, fixed:** after the player died the indicator stayed on screen. `player.gd` switches physics processing off on death, so the per-frame update never ran again. `_on_death` now clears it (H8-182).
2. **Test fixture, not a game bug:** the world's own goblin stands in the lunge strip and out-scores a test stub, so the skill check takes the world's enemies out of the `damageable` group first. The indicator was right to pick it.
3. **Partial runs:** `--only=h8_aim` on its own fails 17 of the targeting checks (H8-33 to H8-61), on your untouched archive as well. In the full run all 80 pass. I did not chase it; it looks like the stand-ins need something an earlier part sets up. The same goes for H8-157 when `h8_indicator` runs alone. Use the full run or `--only=h8_aim,h8_aim_state,h8_touch,h8_indicator` for these.

## Play-test
1. Hold the attack button: a wedge (sword) or a strip (bow) appears round the player, ringing enemies inside it. Slide the thumb: the shape turns with it and the rings go back to plain yellow (no pick).
2. Put a finger on the Lunge button: the strip shows; lift: the lunge goes the way it pointed. Buttons 2 and 3 show nothing.
3. Check the fill and ring strength against your real background; they are `FILL_COLOR`, `EDGE_COLOR`, `CANDIDATE_COLOR`, `PICKED_COLOR` at the top of `AimIndicator2D.gd`.
