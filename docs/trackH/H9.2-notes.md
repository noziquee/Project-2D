## What changed
- `AttackSlash2D.tscn`: the `slash` and `heavy_slash` clips lost their two position tracks (the sprite's `.:position` and
  `HitboxMotion:position`). Frame, scale, fade and the `disabled` hit window are untouched. `RESET` keeps its position
  tracks (it only holds the starting pose).
- `AttackSlash2D.gd`: moves `HitboxMotion` and the sprite along x in code. The hitbox's leading edge (hitbox centre +
  half the hitbox width x the clip's final shape scale + `hitbox_offset.x`) goes from where the scene starts it to the
  attack's reach. Progress is the animation time divided by the end of the hit window (the `disabled` key that turns
  true), eased with `reach_ease` (0.5, the curve the old keys used). The sprite travels the same distance as the hitbox
  (the old keys differed by 2 to 10 px). New: `reach_ease`, `fallback_reach` (70, used when an attack names no reach),
  `hitbox_motion_path`, `is_reach_driven()`, `get_leading_edge()`, `seek_swing(t)`.
- `AttackEffect2D.configure()` takes one more last parameter, `reach` (default 0), stored as `attack_range`.
  `AttackComponent2D` passes `active.get_reach()`. Projectile and area effects ignore it; `SkillComponent2D` is unchanged.
- `Sword_HeavySlash.tres`: fan `max_range` 100 (your decision; this is the `optional-heavy-reach-100` file).
- A slash scene whose clip still animates a position track keeps its own motion (the driver stays off) and
  `get_scene_warnings()` names the clip.
