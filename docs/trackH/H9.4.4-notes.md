# H9.4.4: heavy attacks with auto-approach (D25)

Written and run October 29, 2026: full suite `SUMMARY passes=1632 failures=0` (headless Godot 4.7.2, Linux). Not played in the editor.

## Decision D25 (option A, with release allowed)

A heavy attack asked for while the target is out of reach keeps the charge the player reached, walks to the target, and fires once the target is in reach. The player may let go of the input on the way: the walk goes on and the heavy still fires on arrival.

## Behavior

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Hold `attack_light` past `charge_start_time` (or press `attack_heavy`) with the target out of reach | Walk toward the target, facing the movement direction. The charge does not begin yet; the held time is counted. |
| Let go on the way | The walk goes on. It is not a light tap. |
| Arrive, input still down | The charge begins with the held time and goes on as a normal hold; releasing fires it. |
| Arrive, input already up | The charge begins with the held time and is released the next frame, toward the approach's target. |
| Manual movement, dash, hit stun, a swing or cast, death, target gone or out of view, `max_approach_time`, stall, a weapon with no heavy attack | The approach ends. A held input then charges where the player stands (no restart of the walk until the input comes up), and a released one does nothing. |
| The charge begin is refused on arrival (cooldown ending a few frames late) | It is asked for again for up to 0.5 s (`ARRIVAL_BEGIN_RETRY`). |
| Weapon swap during the walk | The carried charge is dropped when the heavy attack id changes. |

## What changed

- `AttackComponent2D.begin_charge(attack_id, direction, initial_charge = 0.0)`: starts with charge time already built up. A THRESHOLD attack is capped at its threshold.
- `Character2D._get_charge_begin_elapsed()` (new hook, 0 by default), passed to `begin_charge`.
- `player.gd`: `_approach_charge` (held time while walking), `_follow_up_heavy_arrival()`, `_heavy_approach_suppressed`, the arrival branch in `_consume_arrived_approach()`, and the two release branches in `_sample_attack_input()` (a release during a heavy walk is not a tap). `_update_approach()` no longer cancels on release.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: part `h9_heavy`, checks H9-110 to H9-124 (live, on the player in the arena).

## Things to know

- Both shipped heavy attacks are INPUT_RELEASE with no threshold, so the carried charge time changes nothing you can see yet (it is there for a THRESHOLD heavy or a charge bar). What you see: the heavy fires on arrival, and letting go on the way no longer drops it.
- The bow draws (0.5 s) when the charge begins, so on arrival the bow draws first and then shoots. It does not draw while walking.
- A very quick press and release of `attack_heavy` inside one physics frame still does nothing (unchanged).
- A new attack input during the walk replaces it (a fast tap becomes a light approach and drops the carried charge).

## Fixes outside H9.4.4 that the suite needed (the archive lacked them)

- `Sword_HeavySlash.tres`: the fan's `max_range = 100` (the H9.2 decision). Without it the fan defaults to 120 and H9-27 fails. This changes the sword heavy's reach from 120 to 100.
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: H7-64 waits 120 frames (a tap now walks first); `matrix_run` frees the goblin before the skill loop (H7-75). Both were already described in `docs/STATUS.md`.
