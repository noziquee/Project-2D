# H8.8b: ground circle, drag-to-place (October 28, 2026)

Status: built. The shape, the targeting call, the indicator and the touch drag were run headless on Godot 4.7 in a stripped-down project
(26 checks, H8-183 to H8-208, all pass). `player.gd` was only parse-checked against stubs, and checks H8-209 to H8-217 (the player part) and the
full suite have **not been run**. **No skill uses the ground point yet**: `SkillComponent2D.gd` and `SkillDefinition2D.gd` were not in the archive.

## How it behaves
- `GroundCircleAimShape2D` (new): `max_range` = how far out the centre may go, `radius` = the circle's size, `default_pull` = where a tap with no
  target puts it (fraction of the range), `assist_angle` = how wide the target search is round the aim (default 180). Declared kind: `GROUND_CIRCLE`.
- **Drag** on the skill button: the circle's centre goes out along the finger, `pull` of the way to `max_range`. Pull = finger distance from the button
  centre / aim reach (2 button radii), so a finger at the edge of the aim ring is full range. Inside the dead zone there is no manual aim and no pull: assist.
- **Tap**: the circle is put on the best target in the region (held inside the cast range). No target: `default_pull` along the last aim, else the facing.
- **Mouse / controller**: the cursor distance (mouse) or how far the stick is pushed gives the pull. Over the HUD (a hotbar click) there is no pull, so
  the default distance is used.
- **Indicator**: a faint ring for the cast range, a line out to the centre, the area circle there, and rings on the targets it covers.
- The shape guides aiming only. The skill's real area and damage are unchanged.

## Play-test
1. Hold a ground-circle skill button: the cast-range ring and the circle appear. Slide out: the circle moves out with the finger, to full range at about 2 button radii.
2. Drag back to the middle: the circle jumps to the best target (or the default spot).
3. Lift: the skill goes where the circle was.
