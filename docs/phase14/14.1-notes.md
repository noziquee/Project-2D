## What changed
- `Characters/Components/Passives/PassiveDefinition2D.gd` (new): resource with `id`, `display_name`, `trigger` (only `ATTACK_HIT` for now), `required_count`, `counter_window`, `status_effect` (the reward, required) and `consumed_by_next_attack`. `validate()` names the passive in every message.
- `Characters/Components/Passives/PassiveController2D.gd` (new): runtime node. Connects to `attack_started`, `attack_hit`, `attack_finished` (AttackComponent2D), `status_ended` (StatusController2D) and `died` (HealthComponent2D); implements the rules in `14.0-notes.md`. Signals: `passive_counter_changed(id, count, required)`, `passive_triggered(id)`, `passives_changed`. Queries: `get_count`, `get_required`, `is_pending`, `is_active`, `get_passive_ids`; `set_passives()` and `reset()`.
- `WeaponDefinition2D.gd`: the commented-out `passive_skills` array is now real (`@export var passive_skills: Array[PassiveDefinition2D]`), plus `get_passive(id)` and validation (empty entry, each passive's own problems, duplicate ids).
- `WeaponController2D.gd`: `passive_controller` variable; `equip()` calls `set_passives(weapon.passive_skills)` after the skills, so an equip without passives clears the old ones.
- `Character2D.gd`: optional `passive_controller` (a child named `PassiveController2D`), handed to the weapon controller and initialized with the attack component, status controller and health component.
- `player.tscn`: a `PassiveController2D` child (the same way the status controller was added). It does nothing until a weapon has passives.
