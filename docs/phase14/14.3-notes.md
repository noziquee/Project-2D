## Rules (different from the counter passive on purpose)
| Question | Rule |
|---|---|
| Do event passives use the counter? | **No.** They act on events directly; `required_count`, `counter_window` and `consumed_by_next_attack` are ignored. |
| Once per swing or per target? | **Per target hit.** A swing that hits three goblins heals three times and bleeds all three. (Only the counter passive counts once per swing.) |
| Do skills trigger heal or bleed? | **No.** Like the counter, they listen to `attack_hit`, which skills never emit. |
| Does the low-health bonus apply to skills and arrows? | **Yes.** It is read by `CombatResolver2D` for every hit the wielder lands, like the buff multiplier. |
| How do multipliers combine? | They multiply: buff x passive (20 x 1.5 x 1.5 = 45), then crit, then defense. |
| Threshold comparison | The target's `health / max_health` **before** the hit lands, compared with `<=`. |

## What changed
- `PassiveDefinition2D.gd`: new `effect` (`GRANT_STATUS` = the 14.2 behavior and the default, `HEAL_SELF`, `STATUS_ON_TARGET`, `DAMAGE_BONUS_LOW_HEALTH`) and fields `heal_amount`, `health_threshold`, `damage_multiplier`; `validate()` is per effect. Existing passives keep working (Rising Strikes did not change).
- `PassiveController2D.gd`: `HEAL_SELF` heals through `HealthComponent2D.heal()`; `STATUS_ON_TARGET` calls `apply_status` on the hit target's `status_controller` (read by name; a target without one, a freed one or null is ignored); `get_damage_multiplier_against(health)` for the bonus.
- `CombatResolver2D.gd`: step 0 now multiplies the status multiplier by the source's passive multiplier (read from the source's `passive_controller` property, like `status_controller`).
- Data, **on no weapon**: `VampiricStrikesPassiveDefinition.tres` (heal 1 per hit), `SerratedEdgePassiveDefinition.tres` (the existing bleed status on each target), `ExecutionerPassiveDefinition.tres` (x1.5 at or below 30% target health).
- `tests/smoke_checks.gd`: EVT1 to EVT12. EVT9 to EVT11 use real swings on the player.

## Things to know
- **Bleed on hit needs a target that can carry statuses.** The goblins have no `StatusController2D` yet, so in the shipped game the bleed does nothing to them until you add a child node named `StatusController2D` to `BasicEnemy2D.tscn` (the player scene shows how). I did not add it, because bleed ticks would then change goblin health in other tests.
- Expected in the log: nothing new.
- Heal and execute work on the shipped game today; to try one, add its `.tres` to **Passives > Passive Skills** on a weapon.
