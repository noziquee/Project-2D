# 14.0 - Counter rules for passives

Written October 25, 2026, before any Phase 14 code, as the roadmap asks. You said "proceed" to the defaults proposed in chat, so these are the rules 14.1 and 14.2 implement. Change a rule here first if you want it different, then the code.

## Vocabulary
- **Attack (a swing):** the span from one `attack_started` to the next `attack_started` or to `attack_finished`. A combo chain sends `attack_started` for every link and `attack_finished` once, so each link is its own attack. A swing that is cancelled or interrupted sends no `attack_finished` (checked in `AttackComponent2D.cancel()` and `interrupt()`).
- **Qualifying hit:** one `AttackComponent2D.attack_hit`, which comes from a basic or heavy attack effect (melee or the bow's arrow).

## The rules
| Question | Rule |
|---|---|
| Do misses count? | **No.** A swing that hits nothing neither adds to the counter nor resets it. |
| Does a multi-target swing count once per target or once per attack? | **Once per attack.** The first hit of a swing counts; its other hits do not. |
| Do skill hits count? | **No.** Skill hits go through `SkillComponent2D.skill_hit` and never emit `attack_hit`. Status ticks are not attacks either. |
| When does the counter reset? | When it reaches `required_count` (the reward is earned); when `counter_window` seconds pass with no qualifying swing (0 = never); on death; and when the passive list is replaced (an equip). |
| Can rewards overlap? | **No.** While a reward is earned-but-waiting or active, hits do not count. Counting restarts from 0 after the reward ends. |
| When is the reward granted? | When the swing that earned it **closes**, not at the hit. Otherwise the second and third target of the same swing would already be buffed, and the reward would not be "for the next attack". A hit that arrives after its swing closed (an arrow) grants at once. |
| What does "consumed by the next attack" mean? | The next attack that **starts** after the grant uses the reward up: its status is removed when that swing closes, whether it hit or missed. Its hits get the bonus and do not count. With `consumed_by_next_attack` off, the status just runs its own duration. |
| What if the reward is never used? | It ends when the status duration ends (8 s for Rising Strikes). |
| What if the weapon is swapped? | Counters restart and a granted reward is taken away: passives belong to the weapon. |

## Known limits, on purpose
- The status multiplier is read when a hit is **resolved** (13.3.4). An arrow still flying when the consuming swing closes loses the bonus. Fixing it means stamping the multiplier on `HitData` when the effect spawns; not done here, and the first passive is for melee.
- While the reward is active it also boosts any skill used before the consuming attack starts (the multiplier is on the character, not on one attack).
- A late hit (an arrow) that lands after a *new* swing started counts for that new swing.
- Event passives (heal on hit, bleed on hit, bonus damage below a health threshold) have their own rules, written in `14.3-notes.md`.
